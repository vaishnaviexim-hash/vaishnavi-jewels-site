To choose a diamond mangalsutra for daily wear, start with comfort: a fine black-bead chain at a length that sits where you like it, and a pendant small and flat enough not to catch on clothes. Then match the gold colour to the jewellery you already wear, and check the papers: a jewellery grading report for the diamonds and the BIS HUID hallmark on the gold.

## Which chain length suits daily wear?

The chain decides how the mangalsutra feels through the day. Most women choose one of three lengths:

1. **Short (close to the collarbone):** sits neatly above most necklines and suits office wear and western outfits.
2. **Medium (just below the collarbone):** the most common choice; it shows with sarees, kurtas and shirts alike.
3. **Long (towards the chest):** the more traditional look, often worn tucked inside a blouse or kurta.

If you are unsure, try medium first. Because we make jewellery in our own manufacturing unit, we can shorten or lengthen the chain to suit you.

## What kind of chain is best?

For a diamond mangalsutra, a very fine, delicate single-strand chain works best for daily wear. It carries small, even black beads set inline, often separated by tiny gold links, so the chain reads as a fine dotted black line. This kind of chain is light on the neck and sits flat under clothes. Rose or yellow gold chains are chosen to match the pendant.

Check the clasp too. A secure lobster or box clasp that you can open and close easily matters more than people expect when the piece is worn every day.

## How big should the pendant be?

For daily wear, smaller and flatter is usually more comfortable:

- **A single solitaire or a small cluster:** simple and easy with everything.
- **A small geometric or floral motif:** a little more detail without bulk.
- **A slim bar or line of diamonds:** modern and very comfortable under collars.

Keep larger, more detailed pendants for festivals and functions. Many women own two: a light one for every day and a fuller one for occasions.

## Yellow, rose or white gold?

Choose the colour you already wear most. Yellow gold suits traditional wear and gold bangles; rose gold flatters most skin tones and looks modern; white gold suits women who wear mostly diamonds and silver-toned watches. The black beads look good with all three.

## What should I check before I buy?

1. **The diamond report.** Our jewellery comes with a jewellery grading report from IGI, EGL or TGL that states the quality and origin of the diamonds. Small diamonds are described by their total weight, number of pieces and quality.
2. **The BIS HUID.** The gold should carry the BIS mark, the purity grade and a six-character HUID, which you can check yourself in the BIS Care app.
3. **The invoice.** It should list the gold weight and purity and the diamond details.
4. **The setting.** Look at the back of the pendant: the stones should sit firmly with no gaps, so nothing loosens with daily wear.

## How do I care for a mangalsutra I wear every day?

- Take it off for swimming, the gym and heavy housework.
- Keep perfume, hair spray and creams away from it; put it on last.
- Clean it gently with warm water, mild soap and a soft brush, then dry it well.
- Get the clasp and settings checked at the showroom from time to time.
