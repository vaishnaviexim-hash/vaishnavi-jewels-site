NRI families can plan most of their wedding jewellery from abroad: shortlist designs on a WhatsApp video call before the trip, order any custom pieces early, and then see, try and collect everything in Surat during the visit, or have it delivered to a family address in India. Keep the itemised invoice and grading reports with the jewellery when you travel, and check the customs allowance of the country you are flying back to before you leave.

## Why do NRI families shop for wedding jewellery in Surat?

Surat is where much of the world's diamonds are cut and polished, so families find a wide choice of certified diamond jewellery and Polki in one city. Many NRI families from the USA, the UK, the UAE, Canada and Australia also have roots in Gujarat, and plan the wedding shopping around a family trip. At Vaishnavi Jewels we make jewellery in our own manufacturing unit, so a design can be customised and finished within the time of a longer visit if it is ordered early.

## How do I plan before I fly?

Start four to eight weeks before your travel date:

1. **Message us on WhatsApp** with the wedding date, the functions, the outfits and the budget you have in mind.
2. **See a shortlist on a video call.** Our team shows pieces live, with grading report details for diamond pieces, at a time that suits your time zone.
3. **Order custom pieces early.** Custom and bridal pieces take 4 days to 3 weeks from the final design, so order before you fly if you want them ready on arrival.
4. **Book a visit slot** for your first days in Surat, so the shortlisted pieces are ready to try.

## What happens during the visit?

In the showroom you can try the shortlisted pieces with your outfits, see each solitaire under a loupe and match its laser-inscribed number to its IGI, EGL or TGL report, and check the BIS HUID on the gold in the BIS Care app. Payment is by NEFT, UPI or cash. If you cannot come to Surat, we deliver within India with free insured delivery to major Indian cities, so the jewellery can go to a family address before you arrive. We do not ship overseas. See [delivery across India](/delivery-across-india) for details.

## Which papers should I keep for travel?

Keep these together, in your hand luggage:

- **The itemised invoice**, showing each piece with its gold weight and karat, the diamond weight and quality, and the amount paid.
- **The grading reports** for solitaires and diamond pieces, with report numbers that match the laser inscriptions.
- **A note of each HUID**, the 6-character hallmark code on the gold.
- **Clear photos of every piece**, which also help with insurance.

Customs rules on carrying jewellery differ from country to country and change from time to time, and some countries ask travellers to declare jewellery on arrival. Check the current rules with the customs authority of the country you are flying to, and of India if you will travel back with jewellery you brought, before your trip.

## Which pieces do NRI brides usually choose?

Most families plan a set for each function. A Polki set for the wedding ceremony, a diamond necklace or collar for the reception, a diamond mangalsutra for daily wear after the wedding, and a certified solitaire ring are the most common choices. Many brides also want pieces they can wear at work and at events abroad, so lighter diamond earrings, bracelets and pendants are popular. ## How do I make the most of a short trip?

Many NRI families have only a few days in Surat between other wedding errands, so a little planning helps. Fix the shortlist on video calls before you fly and share outfit photos for every function. Book your showroom visit for the first or second day of the trip, so there is time for small changes before you leave. Bring the outfits, or at least the blouses and dupattas, to try the pieces against real fabric. If a family member in India can attend the final fitting or receive the delivery, tell us early. Finally, keep a simple list of every piece, its function and its papers, so packing and travel are calm.
