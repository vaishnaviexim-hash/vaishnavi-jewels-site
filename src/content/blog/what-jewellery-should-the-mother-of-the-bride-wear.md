The mother of the bride or groom looks best in one strong necklace, a little lighter than the bride's set, chosen to suit her saree or lehenga rather than to match the bride. A Polki or diamond necklace with matching earrings, a pair of bangles and a ring is usually enough. Comfort matters as much as looks, because she is on her feet and greeting guests all day.

## Start with the outfit

Rich silk sarees such as Kanjeevaram or Banarasi suit gold-toned Polki or a diamond necklace in yellow gold. Pastel or lighter sarees look graceful with diamonds in white or rose gold. Pick up one colour from the border, such as emerald green or ruby red, in the stones.

## One statement, not many layers

A single necklace, either a choker or a mid-length haar, keeps the look elegant in photos. If the blouse neckline is high, choose a longer necklace; for a lower neckline, a choker or collar necklace fills the space well.

## Comfort through a long day

Choose earrings that are not too heavy or add a support chain, and check that clasps are easy to open and close. Because we make pieces in our own manufacturing unit, we can adjust chain lengths and earring weight so they sit comfortably.

## Coordinate the family, gently

It is lovely when the mothers of the bride and groom wear a similar metal tone, but there is no need to match exactly. Let the bride's set stay the brightest in the family photographs.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should the mother of the bride wear Polki or diamonds?

Either works. Polki suits rich silk sarees and traditional ceremonies; diamonds suit lighter outfits and the reception.

### Can the mother of the bride re-wear her wedding jewellery?

Yes. A diamond or Polki necklace with matching earrings is easy to wear again at family functions and festivals.
