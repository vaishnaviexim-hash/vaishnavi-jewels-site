## Why shortlist before you visit?

Wedding shopping days are long, and a showroom with thousands of designs can feel overwhelming when you only have an afternoon. A short video call first saves that time. You see the styles that suit your lehenga and your budget, you can ask questions calmly from home, and when you come in, the pieces you liked are ready for you. Families who live outside Surat often finish most of the selection this way.

## How does the video call work?

On the call our team holds each choker up to the camera in good light, turns it so you can see the front, the back and the clasp, and places it on a neck display so you can judge the size. You can ask to see a piece next to another, or with a long necklace layered beneath it. We also share photos and short videos on WhatsApp afterwards, so you can show your family.

For diamond pieces, we share the grading report details on WhatsApp so you can see what you are buying. For Polki pieces, we explain the stones and the gold weight shown on the bill. Our [bridal jewellery](/bridal-jewellery) page explains how we plan a complete bridal look.

## Choker or collar: which one should I shortlist?

- **A Polki choker** sits close to the base of the neck and is the traditional choice for the wedding ceremony, often with emeralds or pearls. Browse our [Polki jewellery](/polki-jewellery) range for the styles brides choose most.
- **A diamond collar** sits a little lower and flatter, and suits the reception, gowns and lighter lehengas.
- **A layered look** pairs a choker with a raani haar for a fuller bridal set.

If you are unsure, include one of each in your shortlist and decide when you try them with your outfit.

## Can a shortlisted choker be changed?

Yes. Because we make jewellery ourselves, a design you like can be adjusted: a slightly smaller size for a petite frame, different coloured stones to match your outfit, a lighter build for comfort through long functions, or a different gold karat. Custom and bridal pieces take 4 days to 3 weeks from the final design, so start early if you want changes. If time is short, our diamond and Polki pieces are in ready stock.

## What happens when I visit after the call?

When you come in, the pieces from your shortlist are kept ready, so you can spend your time trying them rather than searching. Bring your outfit, or at least the blouse and dupatta, because a choker looks different against real fabric than on a screen. Try each piece standing in front of a mirror with your hair the way you plan to wear it on the day, and ask a family member to take photos from a little distance, the way guests will see you. We also check the fit at the back of the neck and the weight of the earrings, and note any change you want, such as a shorter length or a different clasp, before anything is finalised.
