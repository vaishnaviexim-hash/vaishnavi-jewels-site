Polki and Jadau are often mentioned in the same breath, and for good reason
— both trace back to royal Rajasthani and Mughal courts, and both remain
staples of Indian bridal jewellery today. But they aren't the same thing.

## What is Polki?

Polki refers to **uncut, natural diamonds** set in gold. Unlike faceted
diamonds, Polki stones are left in their raw, table-cut form, which gives
them a soft, antique glow rather than a sharp, modern sparkle.

## What is Jadau?

Jadau is a **setting technique**, not a stone. It's the traditional method
of embedding stones — often Polki diamonds, but also emeralds, rubies, and
pearls — directly into a gold base using pure gold foil, without any
adhesive or claws.

## Which should you choose?

- Choose **Polki** if you want the classic uncut-diamond look as the focus.
- Choose **Jadau** if you want a richer, more layered piece combining
  multiple stone colours in one setting.
