With a sequin or crystal-embellished net lehenga, keep the jewellery soft and clean: uncut or rose-cut diamonds, one hero necklace and slim earrings. A heavily embellished outfit already reflects a lot of light, so jewellery with a calmer surface and a clear outline stands out better than pieces that try to match the shine. Fewer, well-chosen pieces keep the whole look balanced.

## Why can shiny fabric and jewellery clash?

Sequins and crystals scatter light across the whole outfit. When the jewellery does the same, the eye cannot find a focus, and in photographs the necklace often merges with the blouse. This is the most common problem with glamorous reception looks: too much shine everywhere.

The answer is contrast. Jewellery with a softer glow or a firmer shape gives the eye a place to rest.

## Which diamond and Polki styles work best?

**Uncut diamonds and Polki.** Polki has a soft, glassy glow rather than a sharp flash, which contrasts nicely with sequins. A Polki choker or collar looks rich without competing. See our [Polki jewellery](/polki-jewellery).

**Rose-cut diamonds.** These have a flatter top and fewer facets, giving a gentle, mirror-like look that sits calmly against a crystal-heavy outfit.

**Clean, structured diamond pieces.** If you prefer classic diamonds, choose designs with a defined outline and some open metal, such as a geometric collar or a line necklace, rather than pieces covered edge to edge in small stones. ## How many pieces should I wear?

Think of the outfit as already wearing one layer of jewellery. Then add:

1. **One hero piece:** either a necklace or statement earrings, not both at full size.
2. **A supporting piece:** slim earrings if the necklace leads, or a fine chain if the earrings lead.
3. **A bracelet or a single kada, and one ring.**

Skip large maang tikkas and multiple layered necklaces with heavily embellished outfits. If the blouse is covered in crystals, let the earrings lead and leave the neck almost bare.

## Which metal colour suits sequin and crystal work?

- **Silver sequins and clear crystals:** white diamond settings.
- **Gold sequins:** yellow-gold Polki or yellow-gold diamond settings.
- **Champagne, rose or blush sequins:** rose-gold diamond settings.
- **Coloured sequins:** white diamonds or white Polki, with no extra coloured stones.

Matching the metal to the dominant sequin colour makes the jewellery look like part of the outfit rather than an extra layer.

## Is this look good for a reception?

Sequinned and crystal net lehengas are a favourite for receptions and cocktail nights, where strong indoor lights and cameras are everywhere. Clean jewellery photographs better under these lights. Our guide to [jewellery for a mirror-work or sequin lehenga at the sangeet](/blog/jewellery-for-a-mirror-work-or-sequin-lehenga-sangeet) covers dance-friendly choices too.

## What is a quick way to test the look?

Put on the outfit with the jewellery and take a photo with the flash on, then one without. Flash shows how the pieces will look in reception photographs. If the necklace merges with the blouse in the flash photo, choose a piece with a firmer outline or move the focus to the earrings. Also take a photo from a few steps away: your face, not the jewellery or the fabric, should be the first thing you notice.

## Frequently asked questions

### What jewellery should I wear with a gown for my engagement or cocktail night?

A diamond choker, a solitaire pendant or statement diamond earrings suit most gown necklines. Chokers suit strapless and off-shoulder gowns; pendants suit V-necks.

### How do I match jewellery to a heavily embroidered lehenga?

Pick one hero piece, echo one stone colour from the embroidery, and keep the rest in proportion. Skip extra layers where the work is heaviest.

### What jewellery looks best with an ivory or off-white lehenga?

Ivory is a clean base, so gold-set Polki, uncut diamonds, pearls with emeralds or a diamond choker can lead the look.
