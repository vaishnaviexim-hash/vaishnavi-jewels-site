## Why should I see a solitaire under a loupe before buying?

A solitaire is usually the most important diamond purchase a family makes, often for an engagement or a milestone. A grading report describes the stone, but the report is only useful if you know it belongs to the diamond in front of you. The laser inscription is that link.

Seeing the stone under a loupe lets you:

1. Read the tiny report number inscribed on the girdle (the thin outer edge of the diamond).
2. Confirm it matches the number printed on the report.
3. Look at the stone closely yourself, with the report's clarity and cut details in hand.

## Can I still see the inscription once the stone is set?

Usually, yes. In most solitaire settings the girdle is visible between the prongs, so the inscription can be read under a loupe. With some bezel or very low settings, part of the girdle is covered, so it is best to check the inscription before the stone is set. We are happy to do this with you.

## What else should I check before I pay?

- **The BIS HUID hallmark** on the gold of the ring, which you can verify in the BIS Care app.
- **The invoice:** it should list the report number, the stone details and the gold weight and purity.
- **After-care:** ask about resizing (charged on actuals) and periodic prong checks.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I verify an IGI diamond report online using the number on my diamond?

Read the report number laser-inscribed on the girdle under a loupe, enter it on the laboratory's website, and check that the details match your report.

### How do I know the stone I am buying is a diamond and not moissanite or a simulant?

Ask for the grading report, check that the laser-inscribed number on the stone matches the report under a loupe, and make sure the invoice names the stone. Some simple testers cannot tell moissanite from diamond, so the report and inscription are the reliable proof.

### Which solitaire ring setting is best for daily wear?

A lower setting with sturdy prongs or a bezel catches less on clothes. Try several settings on your own hand before deciding.
