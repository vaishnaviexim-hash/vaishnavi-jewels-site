CVD (chemical vapour deposition) and HPHT (high pressure, high temperature) are the two main ways diamonds are grown in a laboratory. CVD builds the crystal layer by layer from a carbon-rich gas, while HPHT recreates intense pressure and heat around a small diamond seed. Both produce real diamond, and the grading report for a lab-grown diamond states clearly that it is laboratory-grown and often notes the growth method and any treatment after growth.

## What is a CVD lab-grown diamond?

In the CVD method, a thin slice of diamond, called a seed, is placed in a sealed chamber. A gas containing carbon, usually methane mixed with hydrogen, is heated until it breaks down. Carbon atoms then settle on the seed and build up the diamond crystal one layer at a time.

The process takes several weeks. The rough crystal is then cut and polished like any other diamond. Some CVD diamonds receive a further treatment after growth to improve their colour; a good report records this.

## What is an HPHT lab-grown diamond?

In the HPHT method, a small diamond seed is placed with a carbon source and a metal mixture inside a press. The press creates very high pressure and temperature, the carbon dissolves and then crystallises on the seed, growing a new diamond.

HPHT crystals often grow in a different shape from CVD crystals, but once cut and polished they are graded on the same scales of cut, colour, clarity and carat.

## Are CVD and HPHT diamonds real diamonds?

Yes. Both methods produce a crystal of carbon with the same structure, hardness and optical properties as diamond. That is why a lab-grown diamond tests as diamond on a standard tester. What a tester cannot show is how the diamond was formed; for that, you rely on the grading report and the laser inscription.

## Is the growth method written on the grading report?

Grading reports for lab-grown diamonds state that the diamond is laboratory-grown. Many reports also note the growth method (CVD or HPHT) and mention any post-growth treatment in the comments section. The exact wording depends on the laboratory.

When you read your report, look for:

1. **The origin statement:** that the diamond is laboratory-grown.
2. **Growth method:** CVD or HPHT, if listed.
3. **Comments:** any note on post-growth treatment.
4. **The report number:** which should match the number laser-inscribed on the girdle of the stone.
5. **The 4Cs:** carat weight, colour, clarity and cut, plus measurements and shape.

For most buyers, the growth method is a detail to understand rather than a deciding factor. What you see and wear is the cut, colour, clarity and carat of the finished stone, and those are graded on the report in the same way whichever method was used.
