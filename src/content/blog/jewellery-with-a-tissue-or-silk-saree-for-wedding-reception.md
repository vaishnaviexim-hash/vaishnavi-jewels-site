For a wedding reception in a tissue or silk saree, a diamond or light Polki necklace with matching earrings is usually the best choice. A saree drape leaves the neckline open and carries the pallu over one shoulder, so a necklace that sits clear of the pallu and earrings that frame the face give the most balanced look. Add a bracelet and your engagement ring, and you are done.

## Why does a saree need different jewellery from a lehenga?

A lehenga usually has a structured blouse and a dupatta, often pinned on both shoulders or over the head. A saree is different: the pallu crosses the body and falls over one shoulder, leaving one side of the neck and chest open. Tissue sarees also have a soft metallic shine, and silk sarees often have a rich border.

This means:

- Very long necklaces can disappear under the pallu.
- One side of the neckline is always more visible than the other.
- Large earrings show clearly and become an important part of the look.

## Which necklace suits a reception saree?

1. **Diamond choker or collar.** Sits above the pallu and the blouse line, so it is always seen. Works especially well with a boat or round neck blouse.
2. **Mid-length diamond necklace.** Rests on the collarbone and suits a V or sweetheart blouse. Keep the length short enough that the pallu does not cover it.
3. **Light Polki necklace.** If your saree is a rich Banarasi or Kanjeevaram silk with a heavy gold border, a lighter Polki necklace can echo the warmth of the zari.

Avoid stacking several long layers. With a saree, one well-placed necklace is clearer than three.

## How do I match jewellery to tissue sarees?

Tissue sarees in gold, silver, champagne, rose or pastel tones already have a gentle shine. Jewellery should add structure rather than more shine.

- **Gold and champagne tissue:** yellow or rose-gold diamond settings, or light Polki.
- **Silver and grey tissue:** white diamond settings.
- **Pastel tissue:** rose-gold diamonds or pearls with diamonds.

Choose pieces with a clean outline, such as a defined collar or geometric earrings, so they do not blur into the fabric under evening lights.

## How do I match jewellery to silk sarees?

Silk sarees often bring strong colour and a heavy border. Let the border guide the metal: a gold zari border suits yellow-gold Polki or diamond settings; a silver or contrast border suits white settings. If the saree is a deep colour like maroon, bottle green or royal blue, diamonds give a crisp contrast, while Polki gives a warmer, traditional feel.

## Which earrings work best with a saree?

Because one side of the face is always open, earrings carry much of the look. Long diamond drops, chandbalis or statement studs all work. If your necklace is bold, pick slightly smaller earrings; if you skip the necklace, let the earrings be large. A neat bun or side-swept hair shows them off well.

## Frequently asked questions

### What jewellery should I wear with a gown or indo-western outfit for my engagement or cocktail night?

A diamond choker, a solitaire pendant or statement diamond earrings suit most gown necklines. Chokers suit strapless and off-shoulder gowns; pendants suit V-necks.

### Polki or diamond jewellery: what should I wear for the wedding and the reception?

Polki usually suits the wedding ceremony with a heavy lehenga, while diamonds suit the reception in a lighter outfit such as a saree or gown.

### What jewellery does a Gujarati bride wear with a panetar, gharchola or Banarasi saree?

Often a rani haar or long necklace with a choker, a nath, a maang tikka or matha patti, earrings and bangles. Polki and gold-set uncut diamonds suit these sarees well.
