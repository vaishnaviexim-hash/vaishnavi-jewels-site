For a gown or indo-western outfit at your engagement or cocktail night, a diamond choker, a solitaire pendant or a pair of statement diamond earrings suit most looks. Match the necklace to the neckline: chokers and collars for strapless and off-shoulder gowns, pendants for V-necks, and statement earrings with high or halter necks. Add a bracelet and your engagement ring, and keep the rest simple.

## Why do gowns need a different approach from lehengas?

Gowns and indo-western outfits have cleaner lines, more open necklines and less embroidery than bridal lehengas. Heavy traditional sets can look out of place against them. Diamond jewellery with a lighter, more modern design suits the structure of a gown and the bright indoor lighting of an evening event.

## Which necklace suits which neckline?

1. **Strapless or sweetheart:** a diamond choker or collar fills the open space and frames the shoulders.
2. **Off-shoulder:** a choker or a short necklace that sits on the collarbone.
3. **V-neck or deep neck:** a solitaire pendant or a fine Y-shaped necklace that follows the line of the V.
4. **Halter or high neck:** skip the necklace and let statement earrings lead.
5. **One-shoulder:** no necklace, long earrings on the open side, and a bracelet on the bare arm.

## Which earrings work with a gown?

- **Diamond studs or a solitaire pair:** clean and classic, especially with a bold necklace.
- **Long drops or line earrings:** suit high necks and updos.
- **Statement or chandelier earrings:** the main piece when there is no necklace.

Our [diamond earrings](/diamond-earrings) page shows the range. If your hair is open, choose slightly larger earrings so they remain visible.

## Should the engagement ring guide the rest of the jewellery?

It helps. The engagement ring will be in every close-up photograph, so the rest of the set should suit it. If your ring is a white-gold solitaire, white settings for the necklace and earrings keep the look consistent. If it is in yellow or rose gold, match that metal.

Because the ring is the star of an engagement, many brides keep the other jewellery quieter: a fine pendant, small drops and a slim bracelet. ## Can I wear colour with a gown?

Yes, in small amounts. A single emerald, ruby or sapphire-coloured drop on a diamond pendant, or coloured stones in the earrings, can echo the gown colour. With a bold-coloured gown, white diamonds alone usually look cleanest.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which solitaire ring setting is best for daily wear and for a small hand?

A lower setting with sturdy prongs or a bezel catches less on clothes. Oval, pear and marquise shapes make fingers look longer, and a slimmer band suits small hands.

### What jewellery should I wear with a tissue or silk saree for my reception?

A diamond or light Polki necklace with matching earrings suits a saree, sitting clear of the pallu and framing the face.

### Which diamond or Polki jewellery goes with a sequin or crystal net lehenga?

Keep it soft and clean: uncut or rose-cut diamonds, one hero necklace and slim earrings, so the fabric's shine does not compete.
