At Vaishnavi Jewels we accept old gold jewellery, including pieces bought from other jewellers, in exchange towards new jewellery, and because we make jewellery in our own manufacturing unit in Surat, the new piece can be a diamond design of your choice. ## Why do families remake old gold?

Most homes have gold that is no longer worn: a chain with a broken clasp, earrings from childhood, bangles that no longer fit, or a set that feels dated. Turning that gold into a piece you will wear again is a practical way to refresh your jewellery for a wedding, an anniversary or daily wear. Popular choices include a diamond pendant or mangalsutra, a pair of diamond earrings, a solitaire ring or a light diamond necklace.

## How does an old gold exchange work, step by step?

1. **Bring or show your old pieces.** Start on WhatsApp with clear photos, then bring the jewellery to the showroom when you are ready.
2. **Weight and purity.** The gold is weighed and its purity is checked before its value is agreed. Ask to see both numbers, wherever you exchange gold.
3. **Choose the design.** Pick one of our designs, share a photo or sketch of a piece you like, or describe what you want. We can change the gold karat, the diamond quality and the size to suit you.
4. **Agree the details.** The gold, the diamonds and the design are finalised together, and the value of your old gold is set against the new piece.
5. **Making and delivery.** The piece is made in our own manufacturing unit, in 4 days to 3 weeks depending on the design. Collect it at the showroom, or we send it with free insured delivery to major Indian cities.

## What should I check on the new bill?

A clear bill protects you, so look for these on every remake:

- **The weight and karat of the old gold** you gave, shown separately.
- **The new piece's gold weight and karat.** We make diamond jewellery in 9K, 14K and 18K gold.
- **The diamond details:** for a solitaire, the grading report number from IGI, EGL or TGL; for smaller diamonds, the total weight, number of pieces and quality.
- **The BIS HUID** stamped on the new piece, which you can check yourself in the BIS Care app.

If you want to understand the hallmark first, our guide on [how to check gold purity at home](/faq) explains why the HUID is a more dependable check than home tests.

## Which designs work well for a remake?

Almost any design can be made, but a few choices make the most of old gold. A diamond mangalsutra or pendant uses a modest amount of gold and is worn every day. A pair of diamond earrings or a solitaire ring turns several small old pieces into one you will wear often. A light diamond necklace or bracelet suits families who want a fresh piece for a wedding or anniversary. If your old gold is 22K, remember that diamond jewellery is made in 9K, 14K or 18K gold, so ask how the karat of the new piece is shown on the bill. Bring your ideas, and our team will suggest the options that fit your gold and your occasion.
