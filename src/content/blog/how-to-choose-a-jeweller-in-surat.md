Surat cuts and polishes most of the world's diamonds, so there is no shortage of jewellers here. The hard part is knowing which one to trust with a big purchase. Whichever showroom you visit, these seven checks will tell you more than any advertisement.

## 1. Does every diamond come with a grading report?

A grading report from a recognised laboratory (for example IGI or GIA for solitaires, or IGI, EGL or TGL for finished jewellery) lists the diamond's cut, colour, clarity and carat. The report number should be checkable on the laboratory's website. For lab-grown diamonds, the report must clearly say "laboratory-grown".

## 2. Is the gold BIS HUID hallmarked?

Every hallmarked gold piece in India carries a 6-character HUID code. Type it into the free BIS Care app ("Verify HUID") and you will see the karat and the jeweller who registered it. This applies to light pieces too.

## 3. Do you get a written, line-by-line estimate?

A clear estimate shows the gold weight at the day's rate, the making charge, each diamond or stone line, and 3% GST, before you confirm. If the price is only one number, ask for the break-up.

## 4. Are exchange and buy-back terms explained before you pay?

Ask what happens if you want to upgrade or sell the piece later. Good jewellers explain the terms up front and tell you to keep the original bill and the original certificate.

## 5. Can they make or change a design for you?

A jeweller with its own manufacturing can change the gold karat, the diamond quality or the design itself, and should show you a sketch or a 3D (CAD) design to approve before making.

## 6. What do real customers say?

Read the recent Google reviews, not just the star rating. Look for reviews that mention the piece bought, the certificate and how problems were handled.

## 7. How do they handle delivery and after-sales?

If you live outside Surat, ask how the piece is shipped (it should be fully insured) and how resizing, polishing or repairs are handled later.
