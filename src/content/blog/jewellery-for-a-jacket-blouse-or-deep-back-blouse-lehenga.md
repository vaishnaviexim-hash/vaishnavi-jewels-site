With a jacket-style or high-neck blouse, choose a choker or collar necklace that sits just above the neckline, and let statement earrings do the rest. With a deep-back blouse, keep the front light, with a slim choker or a single pendant, and add long earrings or a back chain so the jewellery follows the line of the blouse. In both cases, pick one hero piece so the blouse and the jewellery do not compete.

## Why does the blouse decide the jewellery?

The blouse frames the face and the neck, so it decides where a necklace can sit and be seen. A jacket blouse covers the collarbone, so a long necklace disappears into the fabric. A deep-back blouse opens up the back and often comes with a modest front, so the back becomes part of the look. Choosing jewellery for the blouse, not only for the lehenga colour, is what makes a wedding look feel finished.

1. **A choker or collar necklace** that sits on the neck above the jacket's edge. Polki chokers with emerald or pearl drops suit embroidered jackets; a diamond collar suits a plain or velvet jacket.
2. **Statement earrings** such as chandbalis, jhumkas or long diamond drops, which show clearly because the neck and ears are the open area.

## What jewellery suits a deep-back blouse?

Deep-back blouses usually have a higher or simpler front, which changes the balance:

- **Front:** a slim choker, a delicate diamond necklace or a single pendant keeps the front neat.
- **Back:** a back chain or a necklace with a decorative clasp section that sits on the open back adds detail where people will see it, especially in photographs and during the pheras.
- **Earrings:** long earrings or ear chains carry the eye from the face towards the back.
- **Hair:** an open back looks lovely with a low bun, so the back detail stays visible.

## What about high-neck and boat-neck blouses?

- **High neck:** treat it like a jacket blouse. A choker above the neckline or no necklace at all, with strong earrings, looks deliberate and polished.
- **Boat neck:** a wide, flat collar necklace follows the line of the neckline, or skip the necklace and choose large earrings and a statement ring.

## How do I check the look before the wedding?

Plan a short trial once the blouse is stitched. Wear the blouse with the dupatta pinned the way it will be on the day, put on the choker or collar and the earrings, and look at yourself from the front, the side and the back in a full-length mirror. Ask someone to take photos in daylight and in warm indoor light, as the wedding photographer will. Check that the necklace sits above the jacket edge without riding up when you turn your head, that the back chain lies flat on an open back, and that heavy earrings do not tilt forward. Small changes in length or clasp are easy to make at this stage, and much harder in the last week.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I wear a long necklace with a jacket blouse?

It usually gets lost, because the jacket covers the area where a long necklace falls. If you want one, wear it over the jacket only when the jacket is plain and open at the front.

### What earrings go with a deep-back blouse?

Long drops, chandbalis or ear chains work well, as they lead the eye towards the open back. Keep them in the same metal and stones as your front piece.

### Should the back chain match the necklace?

Yes, ideally. A back piece in the same metal and stone colour as the choker makes the front and the back read as one look.
